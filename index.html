About

Who I am

I'm Hardy — a third-year Electrical Engineering student at UBC, currently splitting my time between a co-op placement and hands-on board design for UBC Solar. I like the layer of engineering where a schematic turns into a physical board: net topology, grounding, signal integrity, and the firmware that has to talk to all of it correctly.

Most of my recent work lives across PCB layout in Altium, STM32-based firmware, and CAN bus systems — with a growing focus on signal processing. I'm working toward internships in California doing PCB design, firmware, or signal processing for hardware-heavy teams.

Outside of coursework and co-op, I build personal hardware projects — a LiDAR-based docking system, a voice-activated assistant, and a RISC-V CPU written in SystemVerilog.
